Understand how your draft is handled

Your argument stays in this browser unless you download a file.

What is stored

The editor stores your current draft in browser local storage. Demo changes use a separate temporary session and never read or replace that draft.

The service worker keeps the editor available after a successful visit.

What is sent

The editor has no accounts, analytics, advertising, trackers, or third-party runtime services. Your text, diagrams, and exported files are not sent by the editor.

Hosting systems may keep short-lived request logs for reliability and security.

Control your data

Download JSON before clearing site data if you want a portable copy. Clearing this site’s browser storage removes the draft and offline files.
