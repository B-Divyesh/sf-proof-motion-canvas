Policy · 27 August 2026

Privacy

Your arguments stay on your device.

What is stored

Proof Motion Canvas stores your current editable document in your browser’s local storage. The offline service worker caches the application shell and generated editorial plate after your first visit.

What is sent

The editor has no account system, analytics, advertising, trackers, or third-party runtime services. We do not receive the text, diagrams, or exported files you create. Hosting infrastructure may retain ordinary short-lived request logs for reliability and security.

Your control

Use “Save JSON” before clearing browser site data if you want to keep a portable copy. Clearing this site’s storage removes the local draft and offline cache. Exported JSON and HTML files remain wherever you saved them.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact privacy@sociobot.in.
